# CTRL+HEART — 40 Scenario Draft v2 (Full Arc, Revised for Variety)

*Working draft. Each slot = 4 tiers (4/3/2/1, high→low Connection). Each entry: [Scenario Type] + her beat (verbal or NOT — many are wordless) + [RTS Effect: target node(s), effect type, specific flavor] + one-line reasoning. Sample content only — final phrasing belongs to the authoring pass.*

**v2 changes from the first draft, per feedback:** Type 1 (direct question) capped hard — used ~10 times across 40, not as default. Every entry states its RTS effect explicitly, and no two adjacent entries repeat the same node+effect combination. Genuine non-verbal beats included: smiling, blushing, looking bored, seeming like she might leave, distracted warmth — not just questions or silence-as-tension.

## What changed structurally in v2

- **Type 1 (direct question) count: down to 10 of 40** (was closer to 20+ in v1), and 5 of those 10 are concentrated in Slots 5, 9, and 10 — the three beats where the content genuinely demands a spoken question (base PRD-named moments). Elsewhere it's compliments, teasing, wordless looks, boredom, restlessness, and pull-back beats.
- **Every entry now states an explicit RTS effect** — target node(s), whether it's a Stress Event or Direct Emotion Push, and roughly how strong. No two *adjacent* entries repeat the same node+effect combination.
- **New beat types actually used this time:** pure wordless warmth/smiling (Slot 4 T3), genuine boredom/yawning (Slot 3 T2), restlessness/might-leave energy (Slots 4 & 7, T1), and a deliberate **zero-event recovery beat** (Slot 6 T2) — matching original PRD §31's "quiet moments allow recovery" principle, which no version of this draft had included until now.
- **Dual-target Social Events appear regularly from Slot 2 onward**, not just late-game — this was another flat spot in v1 (multi-node events were theoretically allowed but never actually used in the draft).
